## v0.58 gameplay validation checklist

1. House and Gas Station evidence can be collected in either order.
2. Mine remains locked until Survey Manifest + Radio Trace are synthesized at the Ranger Case Board and the Maintenance Map is obtained.
3. Forged/remote evidence requests are rejected when the sender is in the wrong scene, too far away, downed, or the target does not exist.
4. Water Sample remains optional but creates a stabilized junction light in the Mine.
5. Upper and Deep Mine support-light circuits are mutually exclusive and shared across co-op peers.
6. Remote Mine power requests are distance-validated by the host.
7. FOOD/WATER/MED no longer apply instantly; items are consumed only after the action channel completes.
8. Taking damage or losing valid footing interrupts the consumable action without consuming the item.
9. Journal/Crafting/Stash/Field Status block movement, desktop camera/actions, and mobile controls.
10. In co-op, Tenant and Darkness Creature cannot begin as overlapping major encounters; ending one creates a recovery window before the next major threat.
11. Existing down/revive/team-wipe and map-transition behavior remains unchanged.
12. Validate the new Mine lights and mobile UI on real Android hardware for readability/performance.
